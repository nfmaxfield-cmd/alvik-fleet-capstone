# Monetization ideas (running list; started 2026-10-21 at Nick's request, from Professor Sodhi's suggestion)

Ideas only, not decisions. Each: who would pay, why, and what it would need.

1. Lab kit licence (universities, robotics courses): Maxfield IRL Tracking + Live lab + the printed
   dock/attachment files, per-lab licence; sells the "calibrates itself, labels itself" teaching demo.
   Needs: installer polish, a course pack (lesson plans built from the manuals), support terms.
2. Labelling-time savings for small vision teams (startups, research groups, factories doing
   inspection): pay per project or seat; the pitch is the time-saved record (seconds per frame, labels
   from machine logs). Needs: the generic studio, import/export to common dataset formats (COCO, YOLO).
3. Machine-log labelling ("outside data") as the differentiator: any machine that logs its own position
   (robot arms, AGVs, conveyors) labels camera footage for free. Plug-ins per machine brand could be
   sold or licensed. Needs: more plug-ins, a documented plug-in kit.
4. Remote live-lab hosting: a hosted relay so a lab can show or run its robots to remote viewers
   (watch links, controlled access); subscription. Needs: the remote-access work below done properly
   (accounts, security, uptime).
5. Industrial pilot (Prysmian-type plants): low-cost cameras + self-calibration to track reels, carts or
   AGVs on a floor; "ordinary equipment on purpose" as the selling point. Needs: a pilot partner,
   safety and IT review.
6. Consulting/integration services around the above (setup days, custom plug-ins).

Open questions: who owns the IP (university policy on student capstone work; check before selling);
open-core (free studio, paid live lab/hosting) vs fully paid; which licence for the current code.
