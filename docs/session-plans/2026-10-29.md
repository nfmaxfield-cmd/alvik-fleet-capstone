# Session plan, Thursday 2026-10-29 (daytime): the board in bright light, with today's camera fixes

**Premise.** On 28 Oct at 16:29, in late-afternoon daylight, the overhead camera saw the robots' bodies in only 3 of
29 checks, and every robot was set aside within 12 minutes. The cause (docs/build-reports/e02-oct28-overhead-contrast.md):
in daylight the robots' tops stood only 12-28 grey levels from the board (24-37 under the lamp at night), and where a
robot had stood when tracking began the tracker saw only pieces of it. That made the first 10 cm crawl read 12-18 cm
and the turn checks misread. The tracker now sees the whole robot there. Replayed on 28 Oct's own pictures, the
overhead now sees the body 97 % of the time (it was 78 %). A lighting check now runs before anything moves. Today
tests that on the real board, in daylight, with the board as it is now (122 x 122 plywood, no boards).

**When:** daytime, ideally the same light as yesterday's 16:29 (sun from the window). **Time:** about 1 h 15 min.

| 1 | Robots on and heard | 5 min |

- `[held] ...: held, not set aside; checked again next time`: one camera's evidence; it stays in.
