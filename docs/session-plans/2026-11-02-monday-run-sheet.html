1. Setup at school (12 min)

1.1 The network (the robots and the Pi on the same network)

  1. USB drive and both cameras into the Pi. Pi into the power strip. Wait 2 minutes.
  2. Laptop: join the school WiFi (or the hotspot, if that was decided). Taskbar Tailscale icon: Connected.
  3. Window: PowerShell on the laptop. Windows key, type powershell, Enter. Type, then Enter: 
    ssh st1maxfini@100.122.222.75
    Pi's password (nothing shows), Enter. You should see a line ending in $: this is the Pi window. No answer in 30 s: wait 2 more minutes, try again.
  4. Switch on Left, Right, Center. Wait 30 s. Pi window, one at a time: 
    cd ~/alvik-fleet-capstone
    ./run-fleet-pi.sh check
    You should see Robots heard: Center, Left, Right and Left: firmware 2026-10-29a, battery NN% for each. Heard: none: switch one off and on, wait 30 s, again; still none = the network blocks them. Battery under 60 %: use Center instead.

Overhead roughly above the table's middle, the whole table plus a hand's width in the picture. Side high at one end or side, looking down across the table, seeing all of it. No cables in the pictures.

2. Camera setup, lighting, warm-up (5 min) — you only click corners

  1. Left, Right, Center on their crosses, switched on, facing away from the audience.
  2. 2 Setup: all three robots ticked; then the warm-up stays ticked.
  3. Click Take pictures (about 10 s; it starts the recorder itself).
  4. In each picture click the table's corners in order: 1 audience side × left end (0,0); 2 the audience side's other end; 3 opposite 1; 4 the left end's far corner. Same corner = same number in both. Wrong click: Undo.
  5. Hands off. Click Measure (robots flash and move). About 1 min (lights flash; each robot rolls 10 cm and back), then the warm-up about 1 min.
  6. Read the line Your table measures L x W cm ...; they agree on the robots within N cm. Good = size within about 2 cm of the tape, cameras within 2 cm. Then one line per side and the warm-up's sides are in the map: nothing to click.
  7. Under Lighting, click Check the lighting:

3. Place the robots, plan, run

  1. Lift Center off the table, leave it on, out of the way (the spare).
  2. Left and Right on their crosses (tape sticks out evenly all round), front (distance sensor) facing away from the audience, straight across. The robots straighten themselves up BEING BUILT.
  3. 5 Mission: Monday's default, then Plan and preview (10-30 s). Finishes in about 2:00 (1:56 on 61×122, 2:16 on 76×137); Closest approach about 10.0 cm.
  4. Go to run. Pill: Mode: Robots. At the table, hand ready. Click Run (robots).

Spare: after Monday's default, set Robots to Left, Center (Center on Right's cross) or Center, Right.

What the audience sees: (1) lights flash one side at a time (which way each faces); (2) each robot straightens up; (3) both mow their end in short lanes, a short pause after each turn; (4) they meet in the middle and circle each other 1.5 times, ending swapped; (5) one steps aside, each mows the other's inner part; (6) Chicken: straight at each other, both swerve at 15 cm, pass about 10 cm apart; (7) spread out and dance until switched off.

Stop at once if a robot heads for an edge under about 5 cm away without turning, the two are within about 5 cm and closing, or one spins without stopping.
