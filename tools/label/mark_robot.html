MIRLT · Mark the robot 

Which stretch?

Each stretch is a piece of a recorded session where the tracker lost a robot. You mark that robot on one picture a second. The page downloads only the stretch you pick (about 100 MB) and keeps your place if you stop.

Ccentre the view on the ring

Put the ring's centre on the centre of the robot itself: its body, not the bright glow its lights throw on the floor next to it. The ring is a robot's size, so a good mark has the ring around the robot's top edge evenly.
